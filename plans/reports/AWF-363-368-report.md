## Scope

This pass fixes post-implementation data-integrity issues without adding Risk
Engine behavior, live activation, Freqtrade-native strategy logic, or control
panel APIs.

Implemented corrections:

- Phase 61-62 import now removes legacy `source_system="autowfo_phase61_62_awf339"` candidate rows when re-run.
- Phase 63 paper import deletes only rows generated by the current `paper_dir` input batch, so partial imports preserve other paper evidence.
- Phase 63 paper trade rows are enriched from read-only Freqtrade SQLite trade rows by `id` for open/close time, rates, profit, direction, and fee facts.
- Phase 63 paper trade identity is stable by `(candidate_id, source_db_path, freqtrade_trade_id)`; cross-day opened/closed summaries merge into one paper trade fact, and legacy summary-scoped IDs are cleaned during re-import.
- Phase 63 paper survival reports expose `minimum_day_count_met` and `blocking_reasons`; verdict allowance is blocked by insufficient days, missing/stale manifest freshness evidence, zero-trade days, missing match-rate evidence, or mixed candidate roles.
- Survival Gate verdict writes require existing `gate_policies.policy_id` and `strategy_candidates.candidate_id` references and remain append-only.
